FAQ

Questions, answered

What is an analog site?

A place on Earth whose conditions resemble another world, used to test equipment and train crews before real missions.

How is the similarity score calculated?

It combines climate, terrain and geology measures against the target world. It is a ranking index for comparing sites, not a validated accuracy figure.

Can I check a place that isn’t on the list?

Yes. Click anywhere on the globe or search a name or coordinates and the app characterizes it on the spot.

What do the Mars and Moon modes do?

You click a point on the planetary globe and the app lists the nearest catalogued features and their closest Earth look-alikes.

Do I need an account?

No. Press Start exploring and the dashboard opens.

What does “measured vs estimated” mean?

Measured values come straight from a data source. Estimated values are modelled or inferred, and are flagged so you can weigh them accordingly.
